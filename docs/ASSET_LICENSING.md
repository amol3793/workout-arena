  color-coded deltoid diagram (shoulder).
- License: Project-owned (original generated work).
- Commercial use: Yes. Redistribution: Yes. Modification: Yes. Attribution: none.
- Self-hosted: Yes (`public/anatomy/`). Date verified: V1 build.
- Notes: Stylized/illustrative anatomy for prototype validation; muscle names are

## Exercise demonstration media — PRIMARY (real human HD VIDEO)
- Asset: HD MP4 exercise demonstration videos (real people), self-hosted in
  `public/exercise-media/` with WebP posters. Currently: lateral-raise.mp4
  (lateral deltoid), reverse-fly.mp4 (posterior deltoid), push-press.mp4
  (anterior deltoid) — covering all three deltoid heads.
- Source: YMove free exercise video library — https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos
- URL fetched from: `https://ymove.app/api/free/<uuid>?type=video` (+ `?type=thumbnail`).
- Type: 720p MP4 video (portrait).
- License: Royalty-free and free for commercial use (per YMove free-library terms).
- Commercial use: Yes (apps, websites, client materials).
- Redistribution: **Reselling/redistributing the clips as a standalone video
  library is NOT permitted.** We embed individual clips only (permitted use).
- Modification: Permitted for use in our content.
- Attribution: Appreciated, not required — we credit "Video: YMove" in-UI and in
  the footer + exercise pages.
- Self-hosted: Yes (downloaded to `public/exercise-media/`). Date verified: V1
  build (URLs returned HTTP 200, valid `ftyp mp42` MP4s).
- Notes: Resolved through `ymoveVideoProvider`; lazy-loaded, plays only in view,
  paused for reduced-motion (poster shown). 12 verified clips are now self-hosted:
  three shoulder movements plus squat, bench press, deadlift, hammer curl,
  machine chest fly, triceps pushdown, leg extension, hack squat and leg curl.
